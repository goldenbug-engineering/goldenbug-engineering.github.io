Get in touch

Found a bug, stuck on something, or have an idea? Email goldenbug.engineering@gmail.com. It helps to say which phone you play on.

Common questions

The frog will not move. Ribbit Rush steers by tilting your phone. If tilting does nothing, your phone's motion sensors may be switched off: on Android, look for a "Sensors off" tile in Quick Settings and turn it off.

How do I unlock hats and other cosmetics? Each one is earned. In the wardrobe, tap a locked item to see what earns it.

Can I see the tips again? Yes: Settings → Reset tutorial.

How do I stop sending play stats? Settings → untick Share anonymous stats. See the privacy policy for what they contain.
